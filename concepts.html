Introduction

Understanding basic cybersecurity terminology makes it easier to discuss digital risks and the ways systems and information can be protected.

1. Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and information from digital attacks, damage, and unauthorized access.

2. Malware

Malware is software designed to harm a device, disrupt its operation, collect information without permission, or provide unauthorized access.

3. Phishing

Phishing is a deceptive technique that uses messages, websites, or other communications to trick people into sharing sensitive information or taking unsafe actions.

4. Encryption

Encryption transforms readable information into an encoded form using a cryptographic method. Authorized parties can recover the information using the required key or process.

5. Firewall

A firewall monitors and controls network traffic according to defined security rules. It can help restrict unwanted connections to or from a system.

6. Authentication

Authentication is the process of verifying that a user, device, or system is who or what it claims to be. Passwords and security keys are examples of authentication methods.

7. Vulnerability

A vulnerability is a weakness in software, hardware, configuration, or a process that could be exploited to affect the security of a system.
