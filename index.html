Welcome to My Research Project

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and information from digital attacks. As more activities move online, understanding cybersecurity is important for individuals and organizations.

This website introduces common cyber threats, methods of protection, and key concepts that help people stay safer in the digital world.

Research Topics

  • Cyber Threats: Learn about malware, phishing, and other online risks.
  • Cybersecurity Protection: Explore passwords, encryption, and security practices.
  • Key Concepts: Understand important cybersecurity terminology.

Key Terms

Cybersecurity: Protecting digital systems and information from unauthorized access or attacks.

Malware: Software designed to harm, disrupt, or gain unauthorized access to a system.

Phishing: A deceptive attempt to obtain sensitive information by pretending to be trustworthy.

Encryption: Transforming information into a form that unauthorized people cannot readily read.
