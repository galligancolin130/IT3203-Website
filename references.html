Introduction

The following sources provide reliable information about cybersecurity threats, security practices, and basic technical concepts. They can also support further research as this project develops.

Research References

  1. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency. Secure Our World. https://www.cisa.gov/secure-our-world

    Provides practical advice about recognizing phishing, using strong passwords, enabling multifactor authentication, and updating software.

  2. National Institute of Standards and Technology. Cybersecurity Framework. https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework

    Explains a framework organizations can use to manage and reduce cybersecurity risks.

  3. Federal Trade Commission. Online Security. FTC Online Security Resources

    Offers consumer guidance about online safety, protecting personal information, and avoiding scams.
